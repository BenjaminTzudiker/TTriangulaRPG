# Ranged Attacks

*Status: Numbers placeholder*

Ranged combat follows the same structure as melee wherever it can.

## Procedure

1. **Declare the target and pay for the shot.** A shot is an action: it costs the weapon's shot cost + the on-turn surcharge (see [[Actions and the On-Turn Surcharge]] and the [[Weapons#Weapon Table|Weapon Table]]). Every shot comes with one free aim die (see [[Aim]]).
   - If you're within an enemy's reach when you declare the shot, that enemy gets an opportunity attack in step 7.
2. **Buy any number of aim steps.**
3. **The target may react** with off-turn movement: dive for cover, close in on you, or back away (see [[Cover]]).
4. **Roll and total:** the weapon's damage, the free aim die and every aim step. Then subtract the range penalty for the distance after the target's reaction (see [[Range Bands]]). Note any critical dice.
5. **The target blocks** with Ranged Defense, or with Melee Defense if you're now within its reach. Cover applies either way.
6. **Resolve:**
   - armor reduces what's left (Piercing and critical dice ignore some of it);
   - the aim limit caps the HP lost at the weapon's damage roll (see [[Aim]]);
   - if any damage is left, add 1 per critical die;
   - the target loses that much HP.
7. **Opportunity attacks:** every enemy whose reach you were in when you declared the shot may now make an opportunity attack against you, as long as it isn't incapacitated (see [[Opportunity Attacks]]).
