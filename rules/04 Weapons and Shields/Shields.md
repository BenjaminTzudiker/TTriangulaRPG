# Shields

*Status: Locked (bonuses, one shield); Exploratory (Strength requirement, equipping and removing, casting)*

- A shield gives **+1 Melee Defense and +1 Ranged Defense**.
- You only benefit from one shield at a time.
- **Strength requirement (2):** −1 Agility and −1 Finesse per point of Strength below 2, applied separately from armor's Strength requirement (see [[Armor]] and [[Strength]]).
- **Equipping and removing:** equipping a shield to a hand is an **action** with a base cost of **5**, and removing one is an **action** with a base cost of **1** (see [[Actions and Escalation]]). Both can be done only on your turn, and a shield can't be dropped freely (see [[Drawing Weapons]]).
- **Spellcasting:** shields don't block spellcasting on their own, though a shield occupies one hand (see [[Magic]]).
