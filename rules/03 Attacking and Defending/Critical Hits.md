# Critical Hits

*Status: Locked*

A critical hit means hitting a vital spot. It comes from skill, not luck.

- **Critical range:** a damage die is critical when it rolls its maximum. Each odd Finesse value widens the range by 1, up to +3. See the [[Quick Reference#Critical Range Table|Critical Range Table]].
- **The defender knows** how many dice were critical before choosing blocks.
- **Each critical die ignores 1 armor,** on top of any Piercing (see [[Weapons#Weapon Properties|Weapon Properties]]).
- **Critical bonus:** if any damage gets past blocks and armor, add **+1 per critical die**. If blocks and armor stop all of it, critical dice add nothing. For a ranged attack, the bonus comes after the aim limit (see [[Aim]]).
- **Aim dice are never critical.** Only a weapon's own damage dice can be.
