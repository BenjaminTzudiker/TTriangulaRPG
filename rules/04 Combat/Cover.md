# Cover

*Status: Numbers placeholder*

Cover is defined much as in 5e. Its bonus applies to whatever defense you use against a ranged attack.

| Cover | Bonus |
|---|---:|
| Half | +1 |
| Three-quarters | +2 |

**Reacting to a shot:** when you're targeted by a ranged attack, you can use off-turn movement after the damage is rolled, before you block (see [[Ranged Attacks#Procedure|Procedure]]). You can dive for cover, close in on the shooter, or back away.
- **Diving for cover:** if you get fully behind cover, it counts as **three-quarters cover** for that attack. A reactive dive helps, but it can't fully negate a shot that's already committed.
- **Moving at all** in reaction to the shot gives you +1 Ranged Defense against that shot (see [[Defenses#Ranged Defense|Ranged Defense]]).
- **Closing in:** if you end within the shooter's reach, you block with Melee Defense (see [[Defenses]]).
- **Backing away:** the range penalty is measured from where you end up (see [[Range Bands]]).

> [!example] Example: Diving for Cover
> An archer targets a warrior standing in the open and rolls damage. The warrior buys off-turn movement and dives behind a stone wall.
>
> The attack was already committed, so the wall counts as three-quarters cover: +2. Moving in reaction to the shot adds +1 more. The warrior's Ranged Defense is 3 higher against this attack.
