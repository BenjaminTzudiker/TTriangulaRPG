# Earlier Candidate Abilities

*Status: Exploratory; not yet assigned to trees*

These were the candidate abilities until 0.11.2, kept here as they were. The ones being tried now are in [[Candidate Abilities]].

- **Heavy Swing:** +1 stamina per attack step to penetrate 2 armor.
- **Feint:** a Cunning contest; if it works, the defender declares blocks first. **TODO:** contests aren't defined yet.
- **Riposte:** an opportunity attack after a perfect block. *The Riposte in [[Candidate Abilities]] is a different ability; this one is kept as it was.*
- **Tactical Withdrawal:** 2 stamina to move before an attack resolves.
- **Press the Advantage, Momentum, Fluid Footwork, Step Through:** names only. **TODO**.

## Shelved 0.11.5–0.11.6 Candidates

Candidates from the November 2026 ability rounds that were tried or considered and then shelved (reasons for the main ones are in [[Not in This Version]]):
- **Quick Reflexes:** at any time, take 2 off an active hold to spend 1 and return 1 to ready stamina.
- **Monk Throw:** move a creature 1 square after stopping its melee attack with perfect blocks alone.
- **Brace:** a hold on an enemy outside your reach also triggers when it enters your reach.
- **Watchful:** act first in a dash phase against any creature you hold.
- **Sticky Hands:** once per round, dash after a held enemy that leaves your reach without provoking from it, keeping your hold.
- **Hamstring:** a light melee weapon's wound cuts the target's Movement by 2 until the end of its next turn.
- **Second Beat:** reuse an opening turn-order jump on later rounds.
- **Unplaced round notes:** Ward, Counter, Bulwark, Slip Past, Overreach, Blood Up, Blood in the Water, Call the Play, Poker Face, Thousand Hands, Charge (pursuit), Blade and Bow, Poised.
