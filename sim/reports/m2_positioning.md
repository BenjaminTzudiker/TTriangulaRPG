# M2 positioning suite

Generated by `se suite positioning`. Each row is one scenario under one RuleSet with one set of agents, 500 trials. Rows in a group share trial seeds, so their differences are paired comparisons on the same dice. 95% intervals: Wilson for rates, normal for means. "Side" is the team the row reports: its win rate, and how many of its creatures are down (incapacitated or dead) and dead when the fight ends. First mover is the first in initiative, over decisive fights. OAs per fight counts every opportunity attack made, by either side; off-turn squares counts both sides too. Every fight here has its first attack in round 1.
