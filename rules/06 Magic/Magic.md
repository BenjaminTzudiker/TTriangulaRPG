# Magic

*Status: Locked (access goal); Numbers placeholder (casting requirements); TODO (everything else)*

Magic should be optional for anyone and never required.

**Casting Requirements** (*Numbers placeholder*):
- A free hand.
- No armor (light, medium or heavy) and no shield, unless an ability says otherwise.
- Each spell states its stamina cost, its strain cost, and how it works.

**Spells run on the same stamina and strain economy as everything else.** Costs vary from spell to spell. Going over your maximum strain applies to spells too.

## Direction Being Explored

*Status: Exploratory; not rules yet*

- **Schools as tracks.** A caster tree is divided into spell schools: the eight 5e schools for a wizard, elements such as fire and ice for a sorcerer. Each school is its own track in the tree. You buy its ranks with Ability Points, and extra spells at a rank you've reached with Skill Points.
- **A casting stat per tree.** Each caster tree has one main casting stat, usually mental. A spell's core effect scales with the casting stat of the tree you cast it from.
- **Riders with their own stat.** Some spells also have a secondary effect that scales with a fixed stat that fits it: an enchantment's extra targets with Cunning, an abjuration's temporary protection with Will. A spell shared between trees then suits some trees better than others; a Cunning bard gets the most from a Cunning-rider enchantment without investing in another attribute. The core effect stays worth casting at a low rider stat.
- **Stats that make sense.** Every stat a spell uses should be narratable, so most or all spellcasting will key off mental stats.
