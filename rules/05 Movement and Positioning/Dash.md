# Dash

*Status: Mechanism open; Numbers placeholder (distance)*

Turns cover a lot of time, and HP is low. Dashing keeps positioning live all round. It lets you:
- cover more ground than your free movement allows;
- back out when your defenses run low;
- make pursuers spend stamina;
- dive for cover.

A **dash** is movement you pay for. It's an **action** (see [[Actions and Escalation]]).

**When:** you can dash
- on your turn, in any order with your free movement and your other actions;
- in the **dash phase** before any turn: your own, the turn after yours, or anyone else's (see [[#The Dash Phase]]); or
- when you're targeted by a ranged attack (see [[Cover]]).

**Distance:** each dash moves you up to 1 square, +1 per odd Agility value (up to 4).

**Cost:** 1 + your escalation (2 + your escalation in heavy armor; see [[Armor]]), like any action.
- Your escalation resets when your turn ends. So nothing you did on your turn makes a dash after it pricier.
- Every dash makes what you do before your next turn ends pricier, that turn's attacks included.
- Leaving a threatened square can still provoke opportunity attacks.

**How many:** each time you're allowed to dash, you can buy any number of dashes, including none. Each one is a separate action.

## The Dash Phase

Before every turn starts, including the first turn of the fight, anyone may dash.
- Anyone may say they want to dash, at any time before the phase ends. They don't say where, and they can't interrupt a dash that's under way.
- Of those who have said so, the one **highest in initiative** goes: it buys any number of dashes, including none.
- Then anyone may say so again, including a creature that has just dashed.
- The phase ends when nobody wants to dash. Then the turn starts.

> [!example] Example: Dashing
> Three enemies stand 7 squares from you and will all act before you. In the dash phase before the first of their turns you dash away twice: 1, then 2 stamina.
>
> Each enemy that wants to reach you now has to dash as well, and each of its dashes makes its attack steps cost more.
