# Defined Terms

| Term | Meaning | Defined in |
|---|---|---|
| Action | A melee attack step, a ranged shot or an aim step. Each costs +1 per earlier action this turn. | [[Actions and the On-Turn Surcharge]] |
| Attack step | One unit of commitment in a melee attack, adding one weapon damage roll. | [[Melee Attacks]] |
| Aim step | Adds 1d6 to a ranged attack's total; it's an action. Every shot also comes with one free aim die. | [[Aim]] |
| Aim limit | The HP a shot takes can't exceed the weapon's damage roll; critical bonuses add on top. | [[Aim]] |
| Band width | How many squares each of a ranged weapon's range bands covers. Each band beyond the first is −2 to the shot's total against a Medium target. | [[Range Bands]] |
| Piercing N | The weapon ignores N armor. | [[Weapons]] |
| On-turn surcharge | +1 per action already taken this turn. | [[Actions and the On-Turn Surcharge]] |
| Off-turn escalation | +1 per earlier off-turn cost (off-turn movement, opportunity attack steps) since your turn began. | [[Off-Turn Escalation]] |
| Perfect block | Subtract your defense; costs 1, 2, 3… within one attack. | [[Blocking]] |
| Grazing block | Subtract your defense; costs 1; 1 damage leaks through. | [[Blocking]] |
| Critical die | A weapon damage die (never an aim die) rolling within its critical range; ignores 1 armor; adds 1 damage if any gets past blocks and armor. | [[Critical Hits]] |
| Threatened square | A square within an enemy's melee reach. | [[Opportunity Attacks]] |
| Off-turn movement | Movement bought after any creature's turn, or when targeted by a ranged attack (after the shooter's aim, before its roll). | [[Off-Turn Movement]] |
| Cover | Half +1, three-quarters +2, to the defense used against a ranged attack. | [[Cover]] |
| System Shock | The dying track below 0 HP. | [[Dying and Death]] |
| Strain | The rest-recovered pool for abilities and spells. | [[Strain]] |
| Core ability | The first purchase in a class tree. | [[Class Trees]] |
| Tier ability | The 1-AP purchase that opens a tree's next tier. | [[Class Trees]] |
