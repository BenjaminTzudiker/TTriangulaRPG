# Starting Combat and Turn Structure

*Status: Exploratory*

1. **Roll initiative:** **2d12 + Cunning** (both placeholders; see [[Cunning]]).
   - **Ties** (placeholder): the higher Agility + Cunning goes first. If that's tied too, the tied creatures roll off: each rolls a d12, the highest goes first, and any still tied roll again.
2. **Take turns in initiative order.** Each turn goes:
   - **Dash phase:** before the turn starts, anyone may dash (see [[Dash#The Dash Phase|The Dash Phase]]). The first turn of the fight has one too.
   - **The turn:** move and take actions in any order. Your movement can be split up around your actions.
   - **End of the turn:** your held stamina comes back and you may set new holds (see [[Holds]]). Then you recover stamina, and your escalation resets (see [[Actions and Escalation]]).

There are no separate action, bonus action and reaction economies. Everything runs on stamina.
