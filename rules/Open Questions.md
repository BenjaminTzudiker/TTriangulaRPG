# Open Questions

**Combat**
- Ranged lethality under the 0.10.0 bows: untested at normal ranges. Two archers shooting one target back to back are the main risk.
- A better name for "action".
- Whether Dodge Defense stays in the game.
- Area attacks, such as a fireball or dragon breath. One idea: measure cover from the blast's origin, and let a dive out of the area count as three-quarters cover.
- An "out of the fight but alive" state above 0 HP.
- Whether stamina is public. Can players see an enemy's exact stamina, a rough band (such as above half, half to a quarter, under a quarter), or nothing? Choosing how much to commit and telegraphing a lethal threat both depend on it. If every attack and block is visible, a careful player can count an enemy's exact stamina anyway, so a band or a hidden number only holds if something else is hidden too.
- How stamina is tracked at the table and on a virtual tabletop, especially with more than one pool per creature (such as hold tokens; see [[Holds]]).
- The TODO details marked through this packet.

**Attributes**
- More combat benefits for Will (beyond the dying roll) and Cunning (beyond initiative). Intelligence's current bonuses act only at character building; its in-play role is expected to come from abilities and spells.
- Strength's nightly HP recovery: its value depends on how many fights fall between rests, and the Strength 0 character heals slowest.

**Effects**
- Conditions: will there be universal conditions like "paralyzed", or will each ability say what it does?
- Control effects and grappling (see [[Conditions and Control Effects]]).

**Characters**
- Skills and their list.
- Proficiency lists.
- The remaining weapons, and armor tables.
- Class trees and their abilities.
- Magic: its scope, spells and progression.

**GM material**
- Monster stat blocks.
- Encounter guidelines.
- A complete combat example.
- A character sheet.
