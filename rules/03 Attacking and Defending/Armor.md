# Armor

*Status: Locked (values; applies after blocks); Exploratory (Agility defense gate, Strength requirements, heavy dash cost)*

Armor reduces injury **after** blocks. It never makes an attack miss.
- It applies to leaked grazing damage and to damage you didn't block.
- Each critical die ignores 1 point of armor, and so does each point of Piercing (see [[Weapons#Weapon Properties|Weapon Properties]]).

Armor shines when you're taking many attacks. Nimble, high-defense builds shine one-on-one.

| Armor | Value | Agility's defense bonus | Strength requirement | Extra |
|---|---:|---|---:|---|
| Light | 1 | Normal (every even Agility: 2, 4, 6) | None | — |
| Medium | 2 | Even Agility, up to your even Strength (+1 at STR 2, +2 at STR 4, +3 at STR 6) | 1 | — |
| Heavy | 3 | Even Agility, up to one less than your even Strength (+1 at STR 4, +2 at STR 6) | 3 | Dashes cost +1 stamina (base 2) |

- **Agility's defense bonus** covers both Melee and Ranged Defense (see [[Defenses]] and [[Ranged Defense]]). In medium and heavy armor you need both even Agility and enough Strength to keep it—low-Strength characters get 0 Agility defense in medium or heavy armor, while strong, agile characters earn it back.
- **Strength requirements:** for each point of Strength below your armor's requirement, **every action you take costs +1 stamina** (see [[Actions and Escalation]]). It never lowers your attributes, and it doesn't affect blocks, free movement, holds or opportunity attacks.
- **Heavy armor dashes:** a dash in heavy armor has a base cost of **2** instead of 1 (see [[Dash]]). If you're also below Strength 3, the Strength-shortfall penalty adds on top of that.
- **Spellcasting** isn't possible in any armor (see [[Magic]]).
- Specific armor or abilities may add other rules.
