# Defined Terms

| Term | Meaning | Defined in |
|---|---|---|
| Action | A melee attack step, a ranged shot, an aim step, a dash, or helping the dying. Each costs its base + your escalation. | [[Actions and Escalation]] |
| Attack step | One unit of commitment in a melee attack, adding one weapon damage roll. | [[Melee Attacks]] |
| Aim step | Adds 1d6 to a ranged attack's total; it's an action. Every shot also comes with one free aim die, also 1d6. | [[Aim]] |
| Aim limit | The HP a shot takes can't exceed the weapon's damage roll; critical bonuses add on top. | [[Aim]] |
| Band width | How many squares each of a ranged weapon's range bands covers. Each band beyond the first is −2 to the shot's total. | [[Range Bands]] |
| Piercing N | The weapon ignores N armor. | [[Weapons]] |
| Escalation | +1 per action you've taken since your last turn ended. It resets when your turn ends. | [[Actions and Escalation]] |
| Perfect block | Subtract your defense; costs 1, 2, 3… within one attack. | [[Blocking]] |
| Grazing block | Subtract your defense; costs 1; 1 of the damage it stopped leaks through. | [[Blocking]] |
| Critical die | A weapon damage die (never an aim die) rolling within its critical range; ignores 1 armor; adds 1 damage if any gets past blocks and armor. | [[Critical Hits]] |
| Threatened square | A square within an enemy's melee reach. | [[Opportunity Attacks]] |
| Opportunity attack cap | The most one opportunity attack can cost: 1, +1 per odd Finesse, plus any stamina you hold on the target. | [[Opportunity Attacks]] |
| Hold | Stamina you put on an enemy at the end of your turn: your held stamina. Leaving a square in your reach costs it 1 + that stamina, and it adds to your opportunity attack against it. It stays held until the end of your next turn. | [[Holds]] |
| Dash | Movement you buy: on your turn, in a dash phase, or when targeted by a ranged attack. It's an action. | [[Dash]] |
| Dash phase | The moment before each turn starts, when anyone may dash. | [[Dash]] |
| Cover | Half +1, three-quarters +2, to the defense used against a ranged attack. | [[Cover]] |
| System Shock | The dying track you enter when damage takes you below 0 HP. | [[Dying and Death]] |
| Strain | The rest-recovered pool for abilities and spells. | [[Strain]] |
| Core ability | The first purchase in a class tree. | [[Class Trees]] |
| Tier ability | The 1-AP purchase that opens a tree's next tier. | [[Class Trees]] |
