# Forced Movement

*Status: Exploratory*

Some abilities move another creature against its will (such as [[Candidate Abilities#Shove|Shove]]).

To move a creature 1 square, put it in an adjacent square it could move into: empty, not a wall or pillar, and not diagonally past the corner of a wall or pillar (see [[Free Movement]]).
- The square can't be closer to you than the square the creature left.
- If there's no valid square, the creature doesn't move.

Being moved isn't moving:
- It doesn't use the creature's Movement and isn't a dash.
- It doesn't provoke opportunity attacks (see [[Opportunity Attacks]]).
- It doesn't trigger or end holds (see [[Holds]]).
