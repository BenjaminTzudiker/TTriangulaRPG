# Ranged Attacks

*Status: Numbers placeholder*

Ranged combat follows the same structure as melee wherever it can.

## Procedure

1. **Declare the target.** If you're within any enemy's reach, every enemy threatening you may now make an opportunity attack against you, before you pay anything (see [[Opportunity Attacks]]). An enemy that holds you can spend its held stamina on it (see [[Holds]]).
   - You block these with whatever you're holding. If you draw a weapon to block them, you give up the shot (see [[Drawing Weapons]]).
2. **Pay for the shot, or cancel it.** A shot is an action: it costs the weapon's shot cost + your escalation (see [[Actions and Escalation]] and the [[Weapons#Weapon Table|Weapon Table]]). Every shot comes with one free aim die (see [[Aim]]). If you cancel, you pay nothing, and it isn't an action.
3. **Buy any number of aim steps.**
4. **Roll and total** the weapon's damage, the free aim die and every aim step. Note any critical dice.
5. **The target may react** with a dash: dive for cover, close in on you, or back away (see [[Cover]]).
6. **Apply the range penalty** for the distance after the target's reaction (see [[Range Bands]]). Then the target blocks, with Ranged Defense (see [[Ranged Defense]]) or, if you're now within its reach, with Melee Defense. Cover applies either way.
7. **Resolve:**
   - armor reduces what's left (Piercing and critical dice ignore some of it);
   - the aim limit caps the HP lost at the weapon's damage roll (see [[Aim]]);
   - if any damage is left, add 1 per critical die;
   - the target loses that much HP.

> [!example] Example: Shooting in Melee
> An archer with a longbow stands next to an orc and declares a shot at a goblin 6 squares away. The orc makes its opportunity attack first, and the archer blocks it with the bow still in hand. Hurt, the archer cancels: it pays nothing, and it can still draw its dagger, since no hand was used for a shot. Had it drawn the dagger to block, it would have given up the shot anyway.
