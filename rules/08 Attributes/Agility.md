# Agility

*Status: Numbers placeholder*

Agility is whole-body movement: balance, speed, evasiveness and cardiovascular endurance.

**Odd values (1, 3, 5):**
- +1 square of free on-turn movement.
- +1 square per dash (see [[Dash]]).

**Even values (2, 4, 6):**
- +1 Melee Defense and +1 Ranged Defense.
- Medium and heavy armor gate this by Strength (see [[Armor]]).

**Initiative ties:** the higher Agility + Cunning goes first (see [[Starting Combat and Turn Structure]]).
