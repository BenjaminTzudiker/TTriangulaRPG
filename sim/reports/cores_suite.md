# Cores suite

Generated by `se suite cores`. Each row is one scenario under one RuleSet with one set of agents, 300 trials. Rows in a group share trial seeds, so their differences are paired comparisons on the same dice. 95% intervals: Wilson for rates, normal for means. "Side" is the team the row reports: its win rate, and how many of its creatures are down (incapacitated or dead) and dead when the fight ends. First mover is the first in initiative, over decisive fights. Per-fight counts (OAs, off-turn squares, shots, dives) include both sides.
