# HP

*Status: Numbers placeholder*

HP represents lasting physical injury. It's deliberately small: one solid stab is meant to take someone out of commission.

**Maximum HP = 8 + 2 per even Strength value.** That's 8, 8, 10, 10, 12, 12 and 14 for Strength 0–6.

HP doesn't increase with level. You recover **1 HP per night of rest, +1 per odd Strength value** (1, 2, 2, 3, 3, 4, 4 for Strength 0–6). Recovery works the same below 0 HP: at −3 HP with Strength 0, a night of rest takes you to −2.

Dropping to 0 HP or below takes you out of the fight (see [[Dying and Death]]). You stay incapacitated until rest brings you back to 1 HP or more.
