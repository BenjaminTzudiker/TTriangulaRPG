# Free Movement

*Status: Locked (free base movement, corners); Numbers placeholder (distances)*

- **Free movement:** each turn you can move up to your **Movement** for free: 5 squares, +1 per odd Agility value (up to 8). Movement shouldn't be discouraged.
  - You can split it up around your actions.
  - Moving doesn't penalize your aim.
- **Going further:** dash (see [[Dash]]).
- **Corners:** you can't move diagonally past the corner of a wall or pillar. If either square beside the diagonal is a wall or pillar, you have to go around, for example through the other square beside it: 2 squares instead of 1. This applies to every square you move, dashes included.
  - Creatures don't block a diagonal, and neither do low obstacles such as a crate or a low wall.
  - This only limits movement. You can still attack, and threaten, diagonally around a corner.

> [!example] Example: Going Around a Corner
> ```text
> ab
> OX
> ```
> O is a wall, and a and b are empty. To get from X to a, you move to b first and then to a: 2 squares, not 1.
