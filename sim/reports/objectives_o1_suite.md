# Objectives suite (O1)

Generated by `se suite objectives`. Each scenario gives one side (the pursuers) an objective; the other side denies it. Rows run each scenario under both agent families with the pursuers ignoring the objective (`kill`, the control: today's agents) or playing it (`first`); deniers always play it. 500 trials per row. Rows of one scenario share trial seeds, so first − kill is a paired difference on the same dice. 95% intervals: Wilson for rates, normal for means and the paired difference. How fights ended: the objective was done, it failed (deadline passed, or too few pursuers left to do it), one side was out before either, or the round cap. Objective readings (control, scoring, counts, deadlines) are stated in each scenario file. The stamp below is the code; each section names the RuleSet its scenario runs.
