# Shields

*Status: Locked (bonuses, one shield); Exploratory (Strength requirement, equipping and removing, casting)*

- A shield gives **+1 Ranged Defense** (requires **Strength 1**) and **+1 Melee Defense** (requires **Strength 2**). Below those values, you don't gain that bonus (see [[Strength]]).
- You only benefit from one shield at a time.
- **Equipping and removing:** equipping a shield to a hand is an **action** with a base cost of **5**, and removing one is an **action** with a base cost of **1** (see [[Actions and Escalation]]). Both can be done only on your turn, and a shield can't be dropped freely (see [[Drawing Weapons]]). Removing a shield frees that hand immediately—you can switch a weapon to a two-handed grip or cast with it right away, though your escalation still rises as normal.
- **Spellcasting:** shields don't block spellcasting on their own, though a shield occupies one hand (see [[Magic]]).
