# Intelligence

*Status: Exploratory*

Intelligence is learning, reasoning, memory and technical understanding.

**Odd values (1, 3, 5):** new disciplines come easier. Each core ability you already own makes other core abilities cost 1 AP more (see [[Class Trees]]). When you count them, leave out one per odd Intelligence value.

| Core ability bought | Intelligence 0 | Intelligence 1–2 | Intelligence 3–4 | Intelligence 5–6 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1st | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| 2nd | 2 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| 3rd | 3 | 2 | 1 | 1 |
| 4th | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 |

**Even values (2, 4, 6):** learning compounds over a career. Gain 1 extra Ability Point at each of these levels:

| Intelligence | Extra AP at levels |
|---|---|
| 2 or more | 10, 40, 70 |
| 4 or more | 20, 50, 80 |
| 6 | 30, 60, 90 |

This applies retroactively: if you raise Intelligence after one of its levels, you gain that AP at once.
