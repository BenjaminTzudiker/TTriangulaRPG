# Range Bands

*Status: Numbers placeholder*

Each ranged weapon lists a **band width** in squares (see the [[Weapons#Weapon Table|Weapon Table]]).

- **No penalty** out to one band width from you.
- **−2 to the shot's total for each further band,** full or partial. The total can't go below 0.
- **Measure the distance** after the target's reaction (see [[Ranged Attacks#Procedure|Procedure]]).
- **No maximum range.** The penalty is the only limit.

**Your band width** is the weapon's, adjusted by:
- **+1 square per odd Finesse value** (+1, +2, +3 at Finesse 1, 3, 5);
- **−1 square per point of Strength below the weapon's Strength requirement.**

If your band width comes to 0 squares or less, you can't shoot that weapon.

> [!example] Example: Range Penalty
> A longbow archer with Finesse 1 has a band width of 3 + 1 = 4 squares.
>
> A target 10 squares away is in the third band (squares 9–12), so the shot's total is reduced by 2 × 2 = **4**.
